# Clarification Closeout — MSTR-000B

**Status:** CLOSED_FOR_PLANNING / FRONTIER_AMENDMENT_RECONCILED

## Founder Goal

MSTR should become the strongest possible tiny/local code-specialized model for taking software direction and building verified working software. General assistant breadth is not the primary target. Integration with WePLD is a priority, while standalone MSTR remains useful.

## Resolved Clarifications

### C-001 — Is absolute frontier-model superiority the release claim?

No. The program goal is aggressive, but release claims remain evidence-bound. MSTR may claim leadership only for measured scopes. The optimization target is highest verified software-building capability inside the universal-laptop envelope.

### C-002 — May code-specialized foundations compete?

Yes. The previous scan heuristic excluding specialized variants is superseded for the new mission. Base/foundation status, rights, Q4/runtime fit and exact evidence still govern admission.

### C-003 — Is Mellum-4B selected?

No. It is a mandatory rescan candidate because its omission reveals a search gap. It receives no favorable treatment.

### C-004 — May new candidate weights be downloaded automatically?

No. Metadata/static qualification is allowed within normal public research boundaries, but any weight access outside the existing frozen T027/T028 authority requires a new exact founder gate.

### C-005 — Does MSTR-000B authorize training?

No. It is pre-training planning/infrastructure. Weight-changing training remains separately founder-authorized.

### C-006 — Does MSTR-000A still have to wait entirely for T034?

No. The old all-or-nothing entry rule is too coarse and live execution already demonstrated drift. Model-independent contract/replay/environment/verifier/trajectory infrastructure may proceed early when exact dependencies are satisfied. Candidate-dependent tournament/convergence work must wait for stable equivalent candidate qualification.

### C-007 — Should the project use one giant training dataset?

No. Data is organized by explicit source class, software-engineering role, difficulty, provenance, contamination status, language/tooling mix, and training stage. Mixtures are experiments, not hidden constants.

### C-008 — Should larger teacher models dominate synthetic data generation?

No. Student self-alignment is first-class. Teachers are bounded rescue/reference sources only when rights permit and outputs pass independent verification.

### C-009 — Should failed trajectories be discarded?

No. They remain evidence and may support preference/recovery/RL research when contracts allow. They are not ordinary positive SFT examples.

### C-010 — Are tests ground truth?

Not automatically. Verifier health is explicit. Weak/disagreeing/broken/leaked evaluators cannot create clean positive training labels.

### C-011 — Is bug repair the main benchmark?

No. Feature implementation, bounded greenfield building, integration, migration, build/tooling, tests, and repeated repository maintenance are required.

### C-012 — Is multi-agent execution the default?

No. Default remains one MSTR builder plus independent deterministic verifier. Extra planners/checkers/subagents must earn their cost.

### C-013 — Is 8K context a weakness to hide with retrieval?

No. MSTR explicitly optimizes tokenizer efficiency, selective retrieval, compact state, and `NO_RETRIEVAL`. Heavy retrieval is not mandatory.

### C-014 — Is BF16/master quality sufficient to advance training?

No. Q4 is release-relevant. Every material weight-changing stage requires Q4 regression before advancing to a more expensive stage.

### C-015 — Is QLoRA or 16-bit LoRA preselected?

No. Equivalent supported arms are preflighted. Framework/model-specific current guidance is revalidated at execution time.

### C-016 — May autoresearch optimize the benchmark itself?

No. Evaluators, hidden tasks, product constraints, rights, and contamination policy are frozen within a campaign.

### C-017 — What is the relationship with WePLD?

WePLD is the primary Half Moon orchestration partner and a full-system score surface. It must not be required for standalone MSTR or used to misattribute harness gains to model weights.

### C-018 — May B013 freeze the candidate pool using only the older canonical scan because B005-B010 are terminal?

No. Historical scan completion proves what was known at that time, not that a later pool freeze is frontier-fresh. B013 must record a current frontier snapshot and explicitly disposition material post-scan releases. A plausible challenger with missing equivalent evidence keeps B013 pending and requires a separately governed refresh amendment.

### C-019 — Is K2 Horizon 3.7B now selected or authorized because it is a mandatory frontier-review input?

No. It is mandatory to review because it materially post-dates the prior scan and plausibly fits the compact coding frontier. Admission, weight access, equivalent qualification, and selection remain separate fail-closed gates.

### C-020 — Is Composer 3 a technical input to this amendment?

No official Cursor Composer 3 specification/release was verified as of the 2026-09-04 research snapshot. The amendment uses only verified official Composer 2/2.5 and related Cursor engineering material. Future Composer releases may enter a later freshness review.

### C-021 — Does sub-Q4 research replace Q4 as the product/promotion anchor?

No. Q4 remains mandatory. Q3/Q2/structured-ternary or Sherry-class approaches are challenger research only. If such a method changes weights, it is a separately authorized material training stage.

### C-022 — Are targeted trajectory feedback, autoinstall, speculative decoding, Uno-style generation, or low-bit kernels selected implementation methods?

No. They are measured downstream research arms where applicable. Exact architecture/framework/runtime compatibility, rights, hardware identity, cost, and product evidence determine admission. This planning amendment authorizes no execution.

### C-023 — May public benchmark scores be headline truth without sealing against solution/history leakage?

No. Public continuity scores remain supporting evidence. Headline claims require controlled future-Git-history, public-solution/network, evaluator/answer, and solution-cache boundaries plus fresh/private evidence where required.
